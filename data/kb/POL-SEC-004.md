# Bank Account and Payout Destination Changes
## Changing payout bank accounts
Payout bank accounts can only be added or changed by an account admin in Settings > Payout Accounts. The change requires two-factor authentication and micro-deposit verification: two deposits under USD 1.00 arrive within 1-2 business days and must be confirmed.

## Requests through support
Support will never change bank details over chat, email or phone. Any such request must be reported to the Security team.

## Viewing payout accounts
Admins can view the current payout accounts in Settings > Payout Accounts. Account numbers are masked except for the last four digits.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for every admin user.
