# Invoice Management and Scheduling
## Invoice states
An invoice moves through Draft, Pending approval, Scheduled, Paid or Cancelled.

## Cancelling a scheduled payment
A scheduled invoice payment can be cancelled in Invoices > Scheduled until 17:00 ET on the business day before its scheduled date. After that it is processing and cannot be cancelled.

## Duplicate invoice detection
PayLedger warns when an invoice has the same vendor, amount and invoice number as another invoice from the last 30 days.
