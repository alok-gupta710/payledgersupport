# ERP Sync Error ERP-401: Authorization Expired
## What it means
ERP-401 means the connection token to NetSuite or QuickBooks has expired.

## How to fix it
1) Go to Integrations. 2) Click Reconnect on the ERP card. 3) Sign in to the ERP as an admin and approve access. 4) Click Retry sync.
