# Vendor Onboarding and Bank Verification
## Adding a vendor
Open Vendors > Add vendor and enter the vendor's legal name, address, tax form and bank details. US vendors provide a W-9 form; foreign vendors provide a W-8BEN form.

## Bank verification
Vendor bank details are verified by micro-deposits or instant verification before the first payment.

## Vendor portal
Invite the vendor to the vendor portal so they can keep their own bank details up to date.

## Changes to vendor bank details
Any change to a vendor's bank details triggers re-verification and a 24-hour hold on payments to that vendor.
