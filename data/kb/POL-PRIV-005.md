# Customer Data Privacy and Retention
## Tenant isolation
Each customer can only access data belonging to their own account (tenant). Support never discloses another customer's transactions, pricing, invoices or identity.

## Data retention
Transaction records are retained for 7 years to meet financial regulations. Personal data of individual users is deleted 90 days after account closure on request.

## Data exports
Admins can export their own account's data from Reports at any time.
