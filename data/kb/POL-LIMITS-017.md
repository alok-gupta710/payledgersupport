# Transaction and Payout Limits
## Daily payout limits by plan
Starter plan: USD 50,000 per day. Growth plan: USD 250,000 per day. Enterprise plan: custom limits set in the contract.

## Per-transaction limit
A single payment on the Starter or Growth plan cannot exceed USD 100,000.

## Increasing a limit
Limit increases are requested through the customer's account manager and take 3-5 business days to review. Support cannot raise limits.
