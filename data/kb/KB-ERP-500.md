# ERP Sync Error ERP-500: ERP Server Error
## What it means
ERP-500 means NetSuite or QuickBooks returned a server error. The problem is on the ERP side.

## How to fix it
Check your ERP's status page and retry the sync later. If the error persists for more than 24 hours, contact support with the sync log ID.
