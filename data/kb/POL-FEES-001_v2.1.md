# Fee Schedule
## Payout fees
Domestic ACH payouts cost USD 0.50 per payout. Same-day ACH payouts cost USD 2.00 per payout. Fees are charged to the funding account on the day the payout is submitted.

## International payouts
International payouts in EUR, GBP and other non-USD currencies cost a flat USD 5.00 per payout plus a 1.0% FX markup on the converted amount. International payouts are sent over SWIFT.

## Card payments
Card payments cost 2.9% per transaction. There is no monthly minimum.

## Failed and returned payments
Failed or returned payments are not charged a fee. The original payout fee is credited back to the funding account.

## Waivers, discounts and pricing changes
Fee waivers and discounts are not offered through support. Pricing changes require a contract amendment through the customer's account manager.
