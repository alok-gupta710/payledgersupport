# User Roles and Access Management
## Roles
Admin: full access, including settings, payout accounts, approval rules and users. Approver: can approve payments and invoices but cannot change settings. Preparer: can create payments and invoices but cannot approve them. Viewer: read-only access, including reports.

## Adding a user
An admin opens Settings > Users > Invite user, enters the person's work email and chooses a role. Invitations expire after 72 hours.

## Removing a user
An admin opens Settings > Users and clicks Deactivate next to the user. Deactivated users lose access immediately.

## Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan.
