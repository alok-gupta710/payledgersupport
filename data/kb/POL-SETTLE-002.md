# Settlement Timelines and SLAs
## Standard ACH
Standard ACH payments settle within 3 business days of submission.

## Same-day ACH and cut-off time
Same-day ACH payments settle the same business day if submitted before the 14:00 ET cut-off. Payments submitted after 14:00 ET settle the next business day.

## International SWIFT payments
International payments sent over SWIFT settle within 5 business days. Intermediary banks can add a further 1 to 2 business days, which is outside PayLedger's control.

## Business days and bank holidays
Business days are Monday to Friday. US Federal Reserve bank holidays are not business days.

## Late payments
A payment still in 'processing' after its SLA must be escalated to the Payments Operations team with the payment ID.
