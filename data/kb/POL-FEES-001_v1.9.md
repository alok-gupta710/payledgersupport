# Fee Schedule
## Payout fees
Domestic ACH payouts cost USD 0.75 per payout. Same-day ACH payouts cost USD 2.50 per payout.

## International payouts
International payouts in EUR, GBP and other non-USD currencies cost a flat USD 7.00 per payout plus a 1.5% FX markup.

## Card payments
Card payments cost 3.1% per transaction.

## Failed and returned payments
Failed payments are charged a USD 2.00 processing fee.
