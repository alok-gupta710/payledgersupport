# Refunds and Duplicate Charges
## Duplicate charges
Duplicate charges are verified by the Billing team. Once verified, the duplicate amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Who can issue refunds
Support agents, including automated assistants, cannot issue refunds. Only the Billing team can approve and issue a refund.

## Card data
Customers must never be asked to share full card numbers. Billing identifies charges by invoice ID and charge ID.
