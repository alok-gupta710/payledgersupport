# Disputes, Fraud and Account Security Incidents
## Who handles these cases
Suspected fraud, unauthorized transactions, account takeover, chargebacks and legal or regulatory complaints are handled only by the Risk and Disputes team. These cases are acknowledged within 1 business day.

## Protecting the account
While a case is investigated, an admin can pause all outgoing payouts in Settings > Security > Freeze payouts.
