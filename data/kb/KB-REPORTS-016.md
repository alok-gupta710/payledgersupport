# Reports and Exports
## Reconciliation report
Open Reports > Reconciliation, choose a date range and export as CSV or XLSX. The report lists payment ID, status, fees and FX for each payment.

## Scheduled reports
Admins can schedule the reconciliation report to be emailed weekly.

## Who can export
Admins and Viewers can export reports.
