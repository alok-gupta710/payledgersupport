# Payment Approval Rules
## Default approval rules
Payments under USD 10,000 need one approver. Payments of USD 10,000 or more need two approvers. Payments above USD 50,000 need two approvers, and one of them must be an admin.

## Separation of duties
The person who prepared a payment cannot approve it.

## Changing approval rules
Only admins can change approval rules, in Settings > Approval Rules. Changes take effect immediately and are recorded in the audit log. Support cannot change approval rules.
